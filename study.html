Important Historical Dates

Explore these 10 important historical events. Click Learn More to read additional information.

World War II

September 1, 1939

World War II began when Germany invaded Poland. It became one of the largest wars in history.

World War II involved many countries around the world. It ended in 1945 after Germany and Japan surrendered.

Abraham Lincoln's Death

April 15, 1865

President Abraham Lincoln died after being assassinated at Ford's Theatre.

Lincoln was the 16th president of the United States. He led the country during the Civil War and supported the abolition of slavery.

Wright Brothers' First Flight

December 17, 1903

The Wright brothers completed the first successful powered airplane flight.

Orville and Wilbur Wright made their flight in Kitty Hawk, North Carolina. The flight lasted about 12 seconds.

American Civil War

April 12, 1861

The American Civil War began when Confederate forces attacked Fort Sumter.

The war was fought between the Union and Confederacy. Major issues included slavery and the power of the federal government.

Women's Right to Vote

August 18, 1920

The 19th Amendment was ratified, protecting women's right to vote.

Women fought for decades to gain voting rights. The 19th Amendment prohibited denying the right to vote based on sex.

World War I

July 28, 1914

World War I began after tensions in Europe led to a major international conflict.

The war involved major world powers and lasted until November 11, 1918.

Pearl Harbor Attack

December 7, 1941

Japan launched a surprise attack on the American naval base at Pearl Harbor, Hawaii.

The attack led the United States to enter World War II the following day.

Spanish-American War

April 21, 1898

The Spanish-American War began as the United States entered a conflict with Spain.

The war lasted until August 1898 and resulted in major changes to American influence overseas.

Slavery Abolished

December 6, 1865

The 13th Amendment was ratified, officially abolishing slavery in the United States.

The amendment ended slavery except as punishment for a crime after conviction.

Great Depression

1929

The Great Depression began, causing a major economic crisis in the United States and worldwide.

Businesses closed, unemployment increased, and many families struggled financially. The Great Depression lasted throughout much of the 1930s.
